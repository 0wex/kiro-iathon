# Requirements: Mapa ciudadano de riesgo (Piura, FEN)

## Introduction

During El Niño (FEN) rains, Piura's neighbours, brigades and local authorities see critical points first (flooded streets, blocked drains, cracked embankments, landslides) but report them through scattered phone calls and chat groups. Nobody has one prioritized list to decide where to send a brigade first. This feature lets anyone in the demo report a critical point with a photo, a description and a location; deterministic rules classify the hazard and its priority; a board lists every report by priority on a map; and Amazon Bedrock generates a contingency plan for any critical point, taking the nearby reports into account.

Primary user: a neighbour or brigade member in Piura reporting a critical point. Secondary user: the district authority reading the prioritized board.

## Requirement 1: Report a critical point

**User story:** As a neighbour in Piura, I want to report a critical point with a photo, a description and its location, so that the authorities know it exists.

Acceptance criteria:

1. WHEN the user submits a description between 10 and 600 characters and a location inside the Piura region THEN the system SHALL store the report and show it at the top of the board result.
2. WHEN the description is shorter than 10 or longer than 600 characters THEN the system SHALL reject the report with a validation error and keep the form filled.
3. WHEN the location is outside the Piura region bounding box THEN the system SHALL reject the report with a validation error.
4. WHEN the user attaches a photo THEN the browser SHALL compress it to a JPEG of at most 700 KB before sending it, and the system SHALL store it and show it in the report.
5. WHEN the photo is not a JPEG, PNG or WebP image THEN the system SHALL reject it.

## Requirement 2: Priority by rules

**User story:** As a brigade coordinator, I want each report classified the moment it arrives, so that the board is always ordered.

Acceptance criteria:

1. WHEN a report is stored THEN the system SHALL classify its hazard kind (inundacion, huayco, deslizamiento, colapso-drenaje, erosion-defensa, otro) and priority (critica, alta, media, baja) with a deterministic keyword rules table.
2. WHEN two reports of the same hazard lie within 500 m of each other THEN the newer one SHALL be raised one priority level (up to critica).

## Requirement 3: Contingency plan with Amazon Bedrock

**User story:** As a district authority, I want a contingency plan for a critical point generated from its report and the reports around it, so that I can act in minutes instead of drafting it by hand.

Acceptance criteria:

1. WHEN the user presses "Generar plan de contingencia" on a report THEN the system SHALL call Amazon Bedrock (Converse API) with the report, its photo and the reports within 2 km, and SHALL show and store a plan in Spanish with: a situation summary (at most 400 characters), 3 to 6 ordered immediate actions, the resources needed, the responsible actors (for example brigada, municipalidad, COER Piura, bomberos) and a message to warn the population (at most 280 characters).
2. WHEN a plan already exists for the report THEN the board SHALL show it without calling Bedrock again, and the user MAY regenerate it.
3. WHEN Bedrock answers with ThrottlingException THEN the system SHALL retry up to 2 more times with exponential backoff.
4. WHEN Bedrock fails, times out after 20 seconds, or answers with an invalid plan THEN the system SHALL return and store a template plan for the hazard kind, marked with source "plantilla", so the flow never breaks.
5. WHEN a plan is shown THEN the board SHALL say whether it was generated by AI ("Generado con IA") or comes from the template, and that it does not replace official INDECI or COER guidance.

## Requirement 4: Prioritized board and map

**User story:** As a district authority, I want every report ordered by priority and drawn on a map, so that I can send brigades to the most urgent point first.

Acceptance criteria:

1. WHEN the board opens THEN the system SHALL list the reports ordered by priority (critica first) and then by newest.
2. WHEN the board opens THEN every report SHALL appear on the map as a marker coloured by priority.
3. WHEN the user selects a report in the list or on the map THEN the board SHALL show its photo, description, hazard kind, priority, time and its contingency plan if one exists.
4. WHEN the demo account is prepared THEN the board SHALL already contain example reports from Piura, labelled as example data.

## Requirement 5: Demo access

1. WHEN a judge opens `/demo` THEN the system SHALL sign in the demo account without asking for credentials and land on the citizen report board.
