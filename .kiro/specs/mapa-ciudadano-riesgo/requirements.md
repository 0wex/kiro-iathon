# Requirements Document

## Introduction

Source: `CANVAS.md` (our five answers) and the track letter in `RETOS.md` (front "Solidaridad y ayuda humanitaria ante el Fenómeno El Niño (FEN)", track "Mapa ciudadano de riesgo", deliverable "reporte de puntos críticos con foto y tablero priorizado").

### Problem

During El Niño (FEN) rains, Piura's critical points (flooded streets, collapsed drains, damaged river defences, huaycos, landslides) are seen first by neighbours and brigades but reported through scattered calls and chat groups. Information is dispersed, citizens have no simple tool to report, and municipalities and Defensa Civil respond late because nobody holds one prioritized view of where to act first.

### Persona

- **Primary user (track):** a neighbour or neighbourhood brigade member in Piura who sees a critical point and wants it attended.
- **Decision maker:** the district municipality or Defensa Civil (COER Piura) coordinator who reads the prioritized board and assigns brigades.

All data in the product is simulated or public: example reports use real public places, never names, emails or phone numbers.

### The user story that works today (end to end)

A neighbour in Piura opens the board, reports a flooded street with a photo and its location on the map, sees it appear on the map and in the list ordered by priority; the district coordinator selects it and presses "Generar plan de contingencia" to get, in seconds, a plan written by Amazon Bedrock from the report, its photo and the nearby reports.

### Next step after the event

Geolocated alerts to neighbours near a critical point, safe zones and evacuation routes on the map, an AI assistant that answers the population in natural language about what to do before, during and after a disaster, report status tracking (reportado, en atención, resuelto), and consolidation with public institutional data (SENAMHI warnings, INDECI emergencies).

## Requirements

### Requirement 1: Report a critical point

**User Story:** As a neighbour in Piura, I want to report a critical point with a photo, a description and its location, so that the authorities know it exists.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the user submits a description between 10 and 600 characters and a location inside the Piura region THEN the system SHALL store the report and show it selected on the board.
2. WHEN the description is shorter than 10 or longer than 600 characters THEN the system SHALL reject the report with a validation error and keep the form filled.
3. WHEN the location is outside the Piura region bounding box THEN the system SHALL reject the report with a validation error.
4. WHEN the user attaches a photo THEN the browser SHALL compress it to a JPEG of at most 600 KB before sending it, and the system SHALL store it and show it in the report.
5. WHEN the photo is not a JPEG, PNG or WebP image THEN the system SHALL reject it.

### Requirement 2: Automatic classification and priority

**User Story:** As a brigade coordinator, I want each report classified the moment it arrives, so that the board is always ordered.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a report is stored THEN the system SHALL classify its hazard kind (inundacion, huayco, deslizamiento, colapso-drenaje, erosion-defensa, otro) and priority (critica, alta, media, baja) with a deterministic keyword rules table.
2. WHEN the description mentions people at risk (trapped, injured, isolated, evacuation) THEN the priority SHALL be critica.
3. WHEN two reports of the same hazard lie within 500 m of each other THEN the newer one SHALL be raised one priority level (up to critica).

### Requirement 3: Contingency plan with Amazon Bedrock

**User Story:** As a district authority, I want a contingency plan for a critical point generated from its report and the reports around it, so that I can act in minutes instead of drafting it by hand.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the user presses "Generar plan de contingencia" on a report THEN the system SHALL call Amazon Bedrock (Converse API) with the report, its photo and the reports within 2 km, and SHALL show and store a plan in Spanish with: a situation summary (at most 400 characters), 3 to 6 ordered immediate actions, the resources needed, the responsible actors (for example brigada, municipalidad, COER Piura, bomberos) and a message to warn the population (at most 280 characters).
2. WHEN a plan already exists for the report THEN the board SHALL show it without calling Bedrock again, and the user MAY regenerate it.
3. WHEN Bedrock answers with ThrottlingException THEN the system SHALL retry up to 2 more times with exponential backoff.
4. WHEN Bedrock fails, times out after 20 seconds, or answers with an invalid plan THEN the system SHALL return and store a template plan for the hazard kind, marked with source "plantilla", so the flow never breaks.
5. WHEN a plan is shown THEN the board SHALL say whether it was generated by AI ("Generado con IA (Amazon Bedrock)") or comes from the template ("Plantilla de respaldo"), and that it does not replace official INDECI or COER Piura guidance.

### Requirement 4: Prioritized board and map

**User Story:** As a district authority, I want every report ordered by priority and drawn on a map, so that I can send brigades to the most urgent point first.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the board opens THEN the system SHALL list the reports ordered by priority (critica first) and then by newest.
2. WHEN the board opens THEN every report SHALL appear on the map as a marker coloured by priority.
3. WHEN the user selects a report in the list or on the map THEN the board SHALL show its photo, description, hazard kind, priority, time and its contingency plan if one exists.
4. WHEN the demo account is prepared THEN the board SHALL already contain example reports from Piura, labelled as example data.

### Requirement 5: Demo access

**User Story:** As a judge, I want to open the product without creating an account, so that I can try the main flow right away.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a judge opens `/demo` THEN the system SHALL sign in the shared demo account without asking for credentials and land on the citizen report board.
